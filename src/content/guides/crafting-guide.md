# Introduction

Crafting in Path of Exile 2 is how you transform good items into great ones. Unlike many RPGs where gear is purely found as loot, POE2's crafting system lets you directly influence item modifiers, creating equipment perfectly suited to your build.

---

# Basic Crafting Methods

## Orb of Alteration
- Rerolls magic item modifiers
- Best for: Starting a craft, rolling specific single mods
- Cost: Very low

## Orb of Augmentation
- Adds a second modifier to magic items
- Best for: Creating two-mod bases
- Cost: Low

## Regal Orb
- Upgrades magic to rare
- Best for: Taking a good two-mod item to rare
- Cost: Moderate

## Chaos Orb
- Rerolls all modifiers on a rare item
- Best for: Quick rolls on desirable bases
- Cost: Moderate (primary currency)

---

# Advanced Crafting

## Essence Crafting
Essences guarantee one specific modifier on an item.

Best uses:
- Adding life to jewelry
- Adding specific resistances
- Creating caster weapons

## Fossil Crafting
Fossils provide modifier weighting that influences outcomes:
- Dense fossils — more defense mods
- Prismatic fossils — more elemental mods
- Jagged fossils — more physical mods

## Harvest Crafting
Harvest offers targeted crafting options:
- Remove/replace specific modifier types
- Sacrifice crafts for currency
- Reforge keeping specific modifiers

---

# Crafting Flowchart

1. **Start with good base** — item level 86+ for endgame
2. **Quality the item** — 20% quality with appropriate currency
3. **Apply essence** — for guaranteed mod
4. **Fill remaining mods** — harvest, exalt, or multimod
5. **Bench craft** — last missing modifier

# FAQ

**What should a beginner craft?** Start with life + resist gear using essences. It's cheap and always useful.

**Is crafting cheaper than buying?** Sometimes yes, sometimes no. Always price check before crafting.
