## Introduction

Welcome to Path of Exile 2, exile! This guide covers everything a new player needs to know to get started on the right foot. We'll cover the basics, common pitfalls, and essential knowledge to make your first playthrough smooth and enjoyable.

## Understanding the Basics

### What is Path of Exile 2?
Path of Exile 2 is an action RPG developed by Grinding Gear Games. It features deep character customization, a massive passive skill tree, and an item system that rewards knowledge and clever thinking.

### The Core Loop

1. **Kill monsters** - Gain experience and loot
2. **Get better gear** - Improve your character
3. **Progress through acts** - Complete the story
4. **Reach maps** - The real endgame begins
5. **Repeat and improve** - The eternal grind

## Tip 1: Resistances Matter

The single most important defensive stat in Path of Exile 2 is elemental resistance. Here's what you need to know:

- **Cap your resists at 75%** - This is non-negotiable
- **Elemental weakness** - Maps can curse you, reducing your resists
- **Chaos resistance** - Important but lower priority early on

> **Tip**: Check your resistances every time you upgrade gear. A single missing resistance can get you killed.

## Tip 2: Life is Your Best Defense

**The Rule of Thumb:**
- By Act 5: 1500+ life
- By Act 10: 3500+ life  
- Early maps: 4500+ life
- Endgame: 5500+ life

**How to Get Life:**
- Life on every piece of gear (except jewelry early on)
- Strength nodes on the passive tree
- Hybrid life/ES bases

## Tip 3: Understand Your Links

Support gems and linked sockets are the foundation of your damage:

**Socket Colors:**
- Red - Strength gems
- Green - Dexterity gems
- Blue - Intelligence gems

**Link Priority:**
Your main damage skill should be in your best-linked item. A 6-link can triple or quadruple your damage.

## Tip 4: Flask Management

Your flasks are your most important active defense:

| Flask | Usage |
|-------|-------|
| Life Flask | Instant healing, bind to 1 |
| Quicksilver | Movement speed, bind to 2 |
| Utility 1 | Defense (Jade/Granite), bind to 3 |
| Utility 2 | Status removal, bind to 4 |

**Important Flask Mods:**
- "Seething" (instant recovery) on life flask
- "of Heat" (freeze immunity)
- "of Staunching" (bleed removal)

## Tip 5: Don't Be Afraid to Trade

The Path of Exile economy is player-driven:

**Trade Essentials:**
- Use the official trade site for all trades
- Check prices before listing items
- Currency exchange is at the NPC vendors
- Never accept unsolicited trade requests

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Ignoring Defenses
Damage is important, but you deal 0 damage when you're dead.

### 2. Following Builds Blindly
Understand WHY a build makes its choices. Blindly following can lead to expensive mistakes.

### 3. Hoarding Currency
Use your currency to improve your character. A stash full of chaos orbs helps nobody.

### 4. Neglecting Movement
Movement skills are essential. Always have a movement skill equipped.

### 5. Overvaluing MF
Magic Find is a trap for new players. Focus on clear speed and survivability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What class should I start with?
Witch and Ranger are the most beginner-friendly classes. Their playstyles are straightforward and forgiving.

### Do I need to follow a build guide?
Yes, especially for your first character. The passive tree has over 1500 nodes - a guide helps you make good choices.

### How important is gear?
Crucial. Your passive tree is 30% of your power, your gear is 70%. Invest in good weapons.

### What's the hardest part of the game?
The transition from campaign to maps (level 65-70) is the biggest difficulty spike. Don't get discouraged!
