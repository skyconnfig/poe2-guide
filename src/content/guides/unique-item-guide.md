# Introduction

Unique items in Path of Exile 2 offer powerful, build-defining effects that can dramatically change how your character plays. Some uniques are excellent for leveling, while others are essential for specific endgame builds. This guide covers the most important uniques to know.

# Build-Enabling Uniques

## Caster Uniques
- **Shavronne's Wrappings** — Chaos damage doesn't bypass ES (Low Life builds)
- **Presence of Chayula** — Stun immunity + chaos resist
- **Atziri's Reign** — Increased area damage

## Attack Uniques
- **Lioneye's Glare** — Powerful bow with accuracy
- **Starforge** — High physical damage sword
- **Voidfletcher** — Void charges for bow builds

## Minion Uniques
- **The Baron** — Strength scaling for minions
- **Fleshcrafter** — Chaos resistance for ES minions
- **Bone Helmet base** — Minion damage implicit

## Defensive Uniques
- **Lightning Coil** — Physical to lightning conversion
- **Belly of the Beast** — Massive life %
- **Kaom's Heart** — Maximum life but no chest sockets

# When To Use Uniques

## Leveling (Act 1-10)
- Use Tabula Rasa for 6-link
- Goldrim to ignore resist gearing
- Wanderlust for fast campaign

## Early Maps
- Replace leveling uniques with rares
- Keep build-enabling uniques
- Upgrade to corrupted versions

## Endgame
- Specialized unique combinations
- Double-influence bases
- Corrupted + implicit uniques

# FAQ

**Should I use uniques while leveling?** Yes, leveling uniques like Goldrim and Wanderlust significantly speed up campaign progression.

**Are all uniques valuable?** No, many uniques are common and worth only 1-2 chaos. Check the trade site before listing.
