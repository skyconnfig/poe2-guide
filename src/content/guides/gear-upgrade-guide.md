# Introduction

Gear progression in Path of Exile 2 follows a distinct curve from the campaign through endgame. Knowing what to look for at each stage — and when to upgrade — dramatically improves your character's power and survivability. This guide walks through optimal gear progression from your first white item to endgame equipment.

---

# Campaign Gear (Act 1-5)

## What Matters
- **Life** — 25+ life per piece
- **Resistances** — prioritize fire and lightning
- **Movement speed** — 15%+ on boots
- **Weapon DPS** — upgrade every 8-10 levels

## What to Ignore
- Energy Shield (too low to matter)
- Chaos resistance (negligible this early)
- Perfect rolls (waste of currency)

## Upgrade Triggers
- Dying frequently → upgrade defensive gear
- Taking too long to kill → upgrade weapon
- Resistances below 30% → upgrade rings/belt

---

# Early Maps (Act 6-10 / Tier 1-5)

## Stat Priorities
1. **Elemental resistances** — cap at 75%
2. **Life** — minimum 300 per piece
3. **Movement speed** — 25%+ on boots
4. **Main damage stat** — increased damage modifiers

## Gear Sources
- Rare drops from mapping
- Essence crafting on good bases
- Basic trade purchases (1-5 chaos)

---

# Mid Maps (Tier 6-10)

## Stat Priorities
1. **Life** — 4500+ total
2. **Resistances** — overcap by 30% for elemental weakness
3. **Damage modifiers** — 30%+ increased damage per piece
4. **Defensive layers** — start investing in block/evasion/ES

## Upgrade Path
| Slot | Investment |
|---|---|
| Weapon | 10-30 chaos |
| Body Armor | 10-20 chaos |
| Accessories | 5-15 chaos |
| Jewels | 5-10 chaos |

---

# Endgame Gear (Tier 11+)

## Stat Priorities
1. **Life** — 5000+ for comfortable mapping
2. **Chaos resistance** — cap at 75%
3. **Damage** — maximize scaling
4. **Utility** — movement speed, flask effect, item rarity

## Advanced Mods
- +1 to skill gems
- Increased critical strike multiplier
- Additional projectiles/chains
- Curse on hit

---

# When to Trade vs Craft

| Scenario | Best Approach |
|---|---|
| Need quick upgrade | Trade |
| Have good base | Craft |
| Specific mod needed | Trade |
| Multiple mods needed | Craft |
| Cheap items (<5c) | Trade |
| Expensive items | Craft |

---

# FAQ

**How often should I upgrade gear?** Every 8-10 levels during campaign, every 15-20 map tiers in endgame.

**Is trading or crafting better for gear?** For most players, trading is more efficient until you learn advanced crafting.
