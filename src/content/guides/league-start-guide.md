# Introduction

League start day in Path of Exile 2 is a race against time. Every hour counts when currency is scarce and the economy is fresh. This guide covers optimal strategies for progression from character creation through red maps within 24 hours.

---

# Pre- League Preparation

## Before Launch
- Plan your build path
- Install loot filters
- Prepare trade macros
- Clear stash tabs

## Build Selection
Choose builds that:
- Function without gear
- Scale well into maps
- Have fast clear speed
- Don't require specific uniques

# Early Currency Strategy

## First 2 Hours
- Pick up scrolls and transmutes
- ID rare items for potential upgrades
- Sell valuable skill gems

## Hours 2-6
- Chaos recipe for early chaos orbs
- Essence farming in acts
- Flask and jewel selling

## Hours 6-12
- Map selling
- Fragment stockpiling
- Early crafting attempts

# Map Progression

## White Maps (Tier 1-5)
- Alch every map
- Complete bonus objectives
- Unlock watchstones

## Yellow Maps (Tier 6-10)
- Chisel white maps before alching
- Use simple scarabs
- Focus on map sustain passives

## Red Maps (Tier 11+)
- Full juice setup
- Scarabs and sextants
- Specialized Atlas tree

---

# Common Mistakes

- **Over-leveling** — Don't farm zones longer than necessary
- **Ignoring flasks** — Upgrade flasks every 10 levels
- **Poor inventory management** — Be selective about loot
- **Skipping labs** — Complete labyrinth for ascendancy points
- **Wrong Atlas pathing** — Plan your tree before mapping

# FAQ

**What's the best league starter build?** Minion Witch or Lightning Arrow Ranger offer the smoothest day 1 experience.

**How much currency should I have entering maps?** Aim for 30-50 chaos orbs to fund early gear upgrades.
