## Introduction

Gold is the lifeblood of Path of Exile 2's economy. Whether you're crafting, trading, or buying endgame gear, having a steady gold income stream is essential. This guide covers the most effective gold farming strategies for every stage of the game.

## Strategy 1: Expedition Farming

Expedition is consistently one of the best gold-per-hour mechanics in PoE 2. Here's how to optimize it:

**Setup Requirements:**
- Atlas passive points in Expedition
- Runic monsters on your map device
- High movement speed boots

**Execution:**
1. Roll maps with "increased pack size" 
2. Use Expedition scarabs for extra runic monsters
3. Focus on "reroll currency" vendors first
4. Sell logbooks in bulk for premium currency

> **Profit Tip**: Gwen's "Tujen" and "Rog" offer the best currency exchange rates. Prioritize their artifacts.

## Strategy 2: Heist Mechanics

Heist offers consistent raw gold drops with minimal competition:

**Build Requirements:**
- High movement speed
- Good single-target damage for guards
- Coordination gems for lockdown reduction

**Best Contracts:**
- Lockpicking (highest raw gold)
- Perception (good for currency)
- Agility (fastest completion)

## Strategy 3: Map Blasting

Sometimes the simplest method is the best. Run juiced maps at maximum speed:

**Atlas Setup:**
- Take all "connected map drop" nodes
- Invest in "increased pack size" passives
- Use Fortune Favors the Brave on map device

**Map Tier Recommendation:**
- Gold/hour peaks at Tier 11-13
- Lower maps = less gold, higher maps = slower clear
- Find your personal efficiency sweet spot

## Strategy 4: Sanctum Running

Sanctum provides excellent currency rewards alongside gold:

**Key Advantages:**
- Consistent gold per room
- No map investment needed
- Potential for major drops

**Room Priority:**
1. Currency rewards
2. Gold rewards  
3. Relic rooms
4. Affliction rooms

## Gear Optimization for Gold Farming

**Movement Speed**: 35%+ on boots is mandatory
**Item Quantity**: Invest in IIQ where possible
**Item Rarity**: Secondary priority after quantity

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much gold per hour is good?
400-600c per hour is average. With optimized strategies, you can reach 800-1000c per hour.

### Is trading better than farming?
For consistent income, farming is better. Trading requires market knowledge and initial capital.

### What's the best build for farming?
Ranger builds with high movement speed and fast clear. Deadeye Lightning Arrow is the top farmer.

### Should I invest in MF gear?
Yes, but only after your build can comfortably clear the content. Never sacrifice clear speed for MF.
