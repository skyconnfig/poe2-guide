# Introduction

Currency in Path of Exile 2 powers your entire endgame progression. From buying gear upgrades to rolling maps and crafting items, having a strong currency generation strategy separates successful exiles from struggling ones.

---

# Understanding Currency Tiers

| Currency | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Chaos Orb | Standard trade currency | Map rolling, crafting |
| Divine Orb | High-tier trade | Meta-crafting, high-end trade |
| Exalted Orb | Premium currency | Major crafts |
| Annulment Orb | Moderate | Remove modifiers |

---

# Best Currency Strategies

## Essence Farming
- Most consistent profit
- Low entry barrier
- Essences sell in bulk for premium prices

## Harvest Crafting
- Lifeforce sells consistently
- Powerful crafting options
- Good synergy with mapping

## Delirium Mapping
- Cluster jewel profits
- Simulacrum splinter stacking
- Scales with investment

## Boss Rushing
- Fragment profits
- Valuable unique drops
- Quick map completions
