# Introduction

Boss fights in Path of Exile 2 are complex encounters with multiple phases, telegraphed attacks, and punishing mechanics. Understanding these patterns transforms impossible fights into manageable challenges. This guide covers strategies for every major boss encounter.

---

# Campaign Boss Tips

## Act Bosses
- **The Count** — Stay mobile, avoid frontal cone
- **Innocent** — Phase transitions create adds, clear them quickly
- **Kitava** — Heart phase requires burst damage, save flasks
- **The Elder** — Portal phase needs fast clear speed

## General Boss Strategy
- Learn telegraph patterns
- Save movement skills for dodge
- Use boss-appropriate flasks
- Keep area clear of adds

# The Elder Fight

**Phase 1:**
- Basic attack patterns
- Spawns portal adds
- Avoid ground degens

**Phase 2:**
- Multiple portals simultaneously
- Increased attack speed
- Area denial mechanics

**Strategy:** Clear portals quickly, maintain distance, save flasks for phase transitions.

---

# The Shaper

**Key Mechanics:**
- Bullet hell balls — dodge consistently
- Slam attack — highly telegraphed
- Portal phase — burst down portals

**Strategy:** Constant movement is essential. Use a Quartz Flask for phasing to avoid being trapped.

---

# The Maven

**Memory Game:**
1. Watch the sequence
2. Stand in correct zones
3. Repeat three times
4. Survive the explosion

**Strategy:** Memorize the pattern and stay calm. Use defensive flasks during the explosion phase.
