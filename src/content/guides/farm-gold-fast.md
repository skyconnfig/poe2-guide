# Introduction

Gold is the lifeblood of Path of Exile 2's economy. Whether you're crafting, trading, or buying endgame gear, having a steady income stream is essential. This guide covers the most effective gold farming strategies for every stage of the game.

---

# Strategy 1: Expedition Farming

Expedition is consistently one of the best gold-per-hour mechanics in POE2.

**Setup:**
- Atlas passives in Expedition
- Runic monster compasses
- High movement speed

**Execution:**
- Roll maps with increased pack size
- Use Expedition scarabs
- Prioritize Tujen and Rog vendors
- Sell logbooks in bulk

---

# Strategy 2: Heist Mechanics

Heist offers consistent raw currency drops.

**Best Contracts:**
- Lockpicking — highest raw gold
- Perception — good for currency
- Agility — fastest completion

---

# Strategy 3: Map Blasting

Run juiced maps at maximum speed:

**Atlas Setup:**
- Connected map drop nodes
- Increased pack size passives
- Fortune Favors the Brave

**Sweet spot:** Tier 11-13 maps for best gold/hour ratio.

---

# Strategy 4: Sanctum Running

Excellent currency rewards alongside gold:
- Consistent gold per room
- No map investment needed
- Potential for major drops
