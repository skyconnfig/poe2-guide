## Introduction

Reaching level 100 in Path of Exile 2 is a significant achievement. This guide breaks down the most efficient leveling strategies from the moment you start to the final push for 100.

## Understanding XP Mechanics

Before we dive into strategies, it's important to understand how XP works in PoE 2:

- **Area Level**: Higher area level = more base XP
- **Pack Size**: More monsters = more XP per map
- **Monster Density**: Some maps have naturally higher density
- **Penalty**: There's an XP penalty for killing monsters 3+ levels below you

## Act Leveling (1-70)

### Best Leveling Zones by Act

| Act | Best Zone | Why |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| Act 1 | The Mud Flats | High density, easy mobs |
| Act 2 | The Chamber of Innocence | Linear layout, great density |
| Act 3 | The Solaris Temple | Huge pack sizes |
| Act 4 | The Harvest | Excellent XP per hour |

### Leveling Tips

**Weapon Progression:**
- Update your weapon every 8-10 levels
- Use movement skills constantly
- Skip side quests that don't offer skill points

## Early Maps (70-90)

### Best Early Mapping Strategy

1. **Run white maps with alchs** - The extra pack size is worth it
2. **Focus on completion bonus** - Each bonus gives 1% quant
3. **Skip hard bosses early** - Don't waste time on tough fights
4. **Use Sacrifice Fragments** - Adds 5% pack size

### Atlas Passives for XP

- **Top Priority**: All "increased pack size" nodes
- **Second Priority**: "Map drop" nodes for sustain
- **Third Priority**: "Boss drop" nodes for currency

## Late Maps (90-97)

### Optimal Strategy

At this stage, you need to balance XP gain with survival:

**Map Rolling:**
- Avoid "reflect" and "no regen" mods
- Prioritize "pack size" above all else
- Keep maps below 80% quant for safety

**Best Map Layouts:**
1. Cemetery - Open layout, excellent density
2. Jungle Valley - Linear, easy clear  
3. Beach - Wide open, fast layouts
4. Strand - The classic linear favorite

## The Final Push (97-100)

### Advanced Strategies

**Rotations:** Join a 6-player rotation for massive XP gains. Each player provides their own map, everyone shares the XP.

**Fog Content:** Expedition and Breach offer the highest density of monsters in the game. Run these exclusively at this stage.

**Safe Play:** Never risk death for more damage. The XP penalty at this level is severe.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does 1-100 take?
An experienced player can reach 100 in ~80-100 hours of playtime. First time players should expect 150+ hours.

### What builds level fastest?
Rangers and Witches have the fastest leveling speed. Melee builds are generally slower for leveling.

### Should I use leveling gear?
Yes. Leveling uniques like Tabula Rasa, Seven-League Step, and Goldrim significantly speed up the process.

### What's the biggest XP waste?
Dying. Each death at level 95+ costs 30-60 minutes of progress. Play safe.
