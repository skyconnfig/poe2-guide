# Introduction

Trading is the backbone of Path of Exile 2's economy. Whether you're buying gear upgrades, selling valuable finds, or flipping currency, understanding the trade system is essential for efficient progression. This guide covers everything from basic trade site usage to advanced trading strategies.

---

# Getting Started

## Trade Site Basics
Official trade site: pathofexile.com/trade2

**Key features:**
- Search items by mods
- Filter by price range
- Compare similar items
- Set up live search alerts

## Premium Stash Tabs
Essential for selling:
- Public premium tab
- Set items as "public"
- Price individually or by bulk

---

# How To Price Items

**Step 1:** Identify the valuable mods on your item
**Step 2:** Search the trade site for similar items
**Step 3:** Compare listings to determine fair price
**Step 4:** Adjust price based on rolls and demand

## Quick Pricing Guide
| Item Type | Base Price |
|---|---|
| Life + 2 resists | 5-20 chaos |
| High DPS weapon | 20-100 chaos |
| +1 skill amulet | 30-200 chaos |
| Perfect rolled unique | 50+ chaos |

---

# Buying Tips

- **Whisper multiple sellers** — increases chance of response
- **Check online status** — filter for online players
- **Bulk buy** — cheaper per unit for currency/maps
- **Negotiate politely** — many sellers accept small discounts
- **Use live search** — catch underpriced items instantly

---

# Selling Tips

| Item | Best Selling Method |
|---|---|
| Currency | Bulk listing |
| Maps | Tab with fixed price |
| Gear | Individual pricing |
| Fragments | Bulk or full set |
| Divination cards | Full stack |

# Currency Flipping

## Low Effience Flipping
- Chaos ↔ Divine at NPC vendors
- Alc and scouring
- Gem cutter's prisms

## Advanced Flipping
- Buy in bulk at discount
- Split into smaller stacks
- Sell individual at premium

---

# Common Trade Scams

- **Wrong item shown** — verify the item before confirming
- **Currency swap** — check currency type before accepting
- **Price bait** — verify item matches what was listed
- **Partial payment** — confirm total before accepting

---

# FAQ

**What should I price my first valuable drop?** Search for identical items on the trade site and price in the middle range.

**How do I avoid scams?** Always hover over the item to verify its stats before confirming the trade window.
