# Introduction

Completing the campaign is just the beginning. Path of Exile 2's true content starts when you enter the Atlas. This guide covers everything you need to know to transition smoothly from campaign completion to endgame mapping and beyond.

---

# First Steps After Campaign

## Immediate Priorities
1. **Cap your resistances** — 75% fire, cold, lightning
2. **Reach 3500+ life** — minimum for early maps
3. **Get a proper 5-link** — or save for a 6-link
4. **Upgrade your weapon** — campaign weapons won't cut it

## Atlas Introduction
- Complete your first map
- Unlock the Atlas passive tree
- Start earning watchstones
- Begin map sustain

# Map Tier Progression

## White Maps (Tier 1-5)
- Focus on bonus objectives
- Collect your first watchstones
- Use alchemy orbs on every map

## Yellow Maps (Tier 6-10)
- Start using scarabs
- Invest in Atlas passives
- Improve gear with map drops

## Red Maps (Tier 11-16)
- Full juice setup
- Optimize your Atlas tree
- Farm for pinnacle boss fragments

# Build Optimization

## Skill Gems
- Level all gems to 20
- Quality gems to 20%
- Consider awakened gems for endgame

## Jewels
- Fill all jewel sockets
- Prioritize life + damage
- Consider cluster jewels

## Flasks
- Life Flask with instant recovery
- Utility flasks with specific immunities
- Increase flask effect where possible

# FAQ

**What should I do first when I hit maps?** Cap your resistances and get a 5-link body armor. Everything else follows.

**How do I sustain maps?** Invest Atlas passives in map drop nodes, always alch your maps, and use cartography scarabs.
