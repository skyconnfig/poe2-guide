# Introduction

The Atlas is Path of Exile 2's endgame mapping system. With hundreds of passive nodes, multiple progression paths, and complex sustain mechanics, a well-planned Atlas strategy is essential for efficient endgame progression. This guide covers everything from your first map to farming pinnacle bosses.

---

# Atlas Progression Path

## Phase 1: White Maps (Tier 1-5)
- Complete each map bonus objective
- Unlock all watchstones
- Focus on map sustain passives first

## Phase 2: Yellow Maps (Tier 6-10)
- Invest in pack size nodes
- Add league mechanics you enjoy
- Begin using scarabs

## Phase 3: Red Maps (Tier 11-16)
- Specialize your Atlas tree
- Invest heavily in preferred content
- Use full sextant and scarab setups

# Best Atlas Trees

## Essence Farming Tree
- All essence nodes
- Connected map drop chance
- Kirac missions

## Expedition Tree
- All Expedition nodes
- Increased logbook drops
- Runic monster upgrades

## Delirium Tree
- Delirium mirror chance
- Cluster jewel nodes
- Simulacrum splinter drops

## General Mapping Tree
- Pack size and quantity
- Map sustain
- Strongboxes

| Strategy | Investment | Sustain Rate |

# FAQ

**How do I unlock all watchstones?** Complete maps at each tier level and defeat conquerors to progress.

**What's the fastest way to sustain maps?** Focus on connected map drop nodes and use cartography scarabs.
