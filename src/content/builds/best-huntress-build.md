# Introduction

The Huntress is Path of Exile 2's precision melee class, wielding spears with deadly accuracy. This build focuses on thrusting attacks and mobility skills to deliver exceptional single-target damage while maintaining strong area clear through skill targeting.

With high critical strike potential and excellent mobility, the Huntress rewards precise positioning and skillful play.

# Gameplay Tips

- Apply Hunter's Mark before engaging tough enemies
- Use Swift Step to reposition behind enemies for bonus damage
- Weave Impaling Thrust and Whirlwind Spear for optimal damage
- Maintain distance and use reach advantage of spears
