# Introduction

The Druid brings a unique shapeshifting mechanic to Path of Exile 2, allowing you to switch between human caster form and powerful werewolf form. This hybrid playstyle offers excellent versatility, strong sustain, and smooth leveling through the campaign.

With powerful area damage in werewolf form and ranged spell options in human form, the Druid adapts to any combat situation.

# Passive Tree Pathing

1. **Physical damage** — scaling for werewolf form
2. **Life regeneration** — sustain during shapeshift
3. **Elemental damage** — hybrid scaling for caster form
4. **Attack speed** — faster werewolf attacks

---

# Gameplay Tips

- Use caster form for ranged engagement, then shapeshift for cleanup
- Regrowth provides excellent sustain during long fights
- Wild Charge is both a movement skill and gap closer
- Coordinate shapeshift cooldowns with enemy attack patterns

---

# Pros and Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Versatile dual-form combat | Requires managing two skill sets |
| Excellent sustain | Gear needs to support both forms |
| Fun shapeshifting mechanic | Lower peak damage than specialists |
| Good survivability | Form-switching can feel clunky initially |

---

# FAQ

**Is Druid hard to play?** It requires managing two forms but is moderately accessible.

**Which form is stronger for leveling?** Werewolf form generally clears faster; caster form is safer against bosses.
