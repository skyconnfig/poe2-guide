# Introduction

The Monk brings a unique hybrid melee-caster playstyle to Path of Exile 2. This Tempest Wind build leverages the Monk's powerful combo system for exceptional clear speed and strong boss damage, making it one of the most engaging classes to level.

With high mobility, strong area damage, and excellent hybrid defenses, the Monk offers a rewarding leveling experience for players who enjoy active combat.

---

# Why This Build Works

The Monk's combo system rewards aggressive gameplay. Building combo points with basic attacks and spending them on powerful wind abilities creates a dynamic combat loop that clears efficiently and kills bosses quickly.

Key strengths:
- Engaging combo-based gameplay
- Excellent AoE clear with tempest skills
- Hybrid Evasion/ES defenses
- High mobility with built-in movement skills

1. **Combo nodes** — increase max combo and combo gain
2. **Elemental damage** — wind and lightning scaling

# Gameplay Tips

- Maintain 5 combo points before spending on finishers
- Use Whirlwind Dash to position behind enemies for bonus damage
- Weave between basic attacks and spenders constantly
- Save defensive cooldowns for boss mechanics
