# Introduction

The Sorceress is Path of Exile 2's elemental damage specialist. This build focuses on cold and lightning spells to deliver massive area damage while maintaining excellent crowd control. With screen-wide clearing potential and strong boss damage, the Sorceress offers one of the most satisfying leveling experiences.

---

# Why This Build Works

Elemental spells scale exceptionally well with gem levels alone, making the Sorceress one of the least gear-dependent classes for leveling. Strong area damage combined with freeze and shock ailments provides both offense and defense.

Key strengths:
- Screen-wide area damage
- Excellent crowd control via freeze
- Low gear dependency
- Strong mana sustain mechanics

# Passive Tree Pathing

1. **Elemental damage** — cold and lightning nodes first
2. **Spell crit** — critical strike chance and multiplier
3. **Mana regeneration** — sustain for continuous casting
4. **Energy Shield** — hybrid life/ES defense

---

# Gameplay Tips

- Pre-stack Arcane Tempest before large packs
- Use Frost Nova to freeze dangerous enemies
- Maintain distance and use Flame Dash for repositioning
- Invest in mana regeneration early for smooth casting
