# Introduction

The Warrior is Path of Exile 2's premier melee class, delivering devastating slam attacks that shake the screen. This Earth Shatter build combines high survivability with massive area damage, making it one of the tankiest leveling options available.

With strong defensive layers and impressive area coverage, the Warrior offers a forgiving leveling experience that excels in hardcore gameplay.

---

# Why This Build Works

Slam skills provide excellent area coverage while the Warrior's inherent tankiness allows for aggressive play. High base life and armor scaling make this build extremely forgiving during the campaign.

Key strengths:
- Highest survivability among all classes
- Massive AoE clear with slams
- Excellent stun and crowd control
- Very forgiving playstyle
- Strong hardcore viability

# Passive Tree Pathing

1. **Two-handed damage** — all two-handed and mace nodes
2. **Life** — at least 180% increased life
3. **Armor** — physical damage reduction
4. **Stun** — increased stun duration and effectiveness

---

# Gameplay Tips

- Pre-cast Seismic Cry before engaging packs
- Use Leap Slam to close distance and as a movement skill
- Maintain Enduring Cry charges during tough fights
- Position slams to hit maximum number of enemies
