# Introduction

The Ranger is one of the fastest leveling classes in Path of Exile 2. This Lightning Arrow build provides exceptional clear speed from the very first act, making it an ideal choice for both league starters and experienced players looking for fast campaign progression.

With high mobility, excellent area damage, and strong single-target potential, this build offers a smooth and rewarding leveling experience that scales well into early endgame content.

---

# Why This Build Works

Lightning Arrow provides outstanding screen-wide clear with minimal investment. Combined with powerful support gems and proper gear progression, this build maintains high clear speed throughout the entire campaign.

Key advantages:
- Fastest campaign clear among all classes
- Excellent mobility with Dash and movement speed passives
- Strong single-target via Ballista support
- Scales smoothly into maps without major respec

---

# Passive Tree Pathing

1. **Bow damage nodes** — all bow attack nodes first
2. **Elemental damage** — lightning and cold damage scaling
3. **Attack speed** — faster attacks = more damage
4. **Life nodes** — minimum 140% increased life by level 80
5. **Ailment nodes** — shock effect and duration

---

# Gameplay Tips

- Keep moving between packs — Lightning Arrow chains behind you
- Drop Ballista Totems before engaging tough enemies
- Use Quicksilver Flask for permanent speed boost
- Dodge roll through telegraphed attacks
