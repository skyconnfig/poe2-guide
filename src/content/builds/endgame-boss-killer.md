# Introduction

After completing the campaign, the real challenge begins. Pinnacle bosses in Path of Exile 2 require specialized builds with exceptional single-target damage and robust defensive layers. This guide covers the best boss-killing builds and strategies for endgame content.

---

# Top Boss Killer Builds

## 1. Crit Spellcaster (Sorceress/Witch)
- Highest burst damage potential
- Uses Elemental Overload or crit scaling
- Requires good positioning
- Best for: Speed farming bosses

## 2. Minion Army (Witch)
- Safest bossing playstyle
- Minions absorb mechanics
- Consistent sustained damage
- Best for: Learning boss mechanics

## 3. Spear Huntress
- Exceptional single-target focus
- High mobility for dodging
- Strong bleed/poison scaling
- Best for: Experienced players

# Defensive Layers

Effective boss killers balance offense with defense:

- **75% elemental resistances** — absolute minimum
- **Chaos resistance** — highly recommended
- **Spell suppression** — crucial for spell-based bosses
- **Life pool** — 4500+ for mapping, 5000+ for pinnacle
- **Guard skill** — Steelskin, Molten Shell, or similar

# Boss Fight Tips

- **Learn attack patterns** — Each boss has telegraphed abilities
- **Phase management** — Save burst for vulnerable phases
- **Flask timing** — Use defensive flasks during dangerous mechanics
- **Positioning** — Stay at optimal range for your build
- **Panic button** — Keep a Portal Scroll available always
