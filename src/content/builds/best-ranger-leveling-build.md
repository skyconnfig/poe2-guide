## Build Overview

The Ranger in Path of Exile 2 excels at fast clearing and excellent mobility. This Lightning Arrow build provides smooth leveling from the coast to red maps with minimal gear investment.

**Key Strengths:**
- Lightning-fast clear speed
- Excellent mobility with movement skills
- Strong single-target with Ballista
- Great for farming currency

**Core Playstyle:**
Shoot Lightning Arrow to clear packs, drop Ballista Totems on rares and bosses, and use your movement skills to stay ahead of danger.

## Skill Setup

### Act 1-3 Skills

| Slot | Skill Gem | Support Gems |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Main | Lightning Arrow | Elemental Damage, Attack Speed |
| Single Target | Shrapnel Ballista | Ballista Support, Faster Attacks |
| Movement | Dash | Second Wind, Increased Duration |
| Aura | Wrath | Generosity, Increased AoE |

### Act 4+ Upgrades

- Switch to **Storm Rain** for bossing
- Add **Galvanic Arrow** as a second clear skill
- Use **Mark** skills for extra single-target damage

## Passive Tree Priority

1. **Bow damage** - All bow attack nodes first
2. **Elemental damage** - Lightning and cold nodes
3. **Attack speed** - Faster attacks = more damage
4. **Life nodes** - Minimum 140% increased life
5. **Ailment nodes** - Shock effect and duration

## Gear Recommendations

### Weapons
- **Early (1-40):** Short Bow with added lightning damage
- **Mid (40-60):** Decimation Bow with high physical DPS
- **Late (60+):** Harbinger Bow with crit chance and multi

### Armor
- Evasion bases are essential
- Look for "movement speed" on boots
- "Attack speed" on gloves is a top priority

### Accessories
- Quiver with added lightning damage to attacks
- Rings with elemental damage and life
- Amulet with crit multi

## Gameplay Tips

### Clear Strategy
Always keep moving between packs. Lightning Arrow chains will clear behind you. Use the "run and gun" playstyle.

### Flask Management
Use a Quicksilver Flask of Acceleration for speed. Keep a Life Flask with "bleeding removal" at all times.

### Boss Strategy
- Drop 2-3 Ballista Totems before engaging
- Keep your distance and dodge roll through attacks
- Use movement flasks to create space

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the best ascendancy?
Deadeye offers the most damage and clear speed. Pathfinder is better for bossing and survivability.

### Can I league start this build?
Absolutely. The build requires zero uniques to function. All gear is self-found friendly.

### Is this build HC viable?
Yes, with proper life investment and defensive flasks. Use a Jade Flask and Granite Flask for extra defense.

### What map mods to avoid?
"No leech" and "elemental reflect" are dangerous. Avoid "less recovery" for comfortable mapping.
