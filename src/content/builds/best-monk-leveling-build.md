## Build Overview

The Monk in Path of Exile 2 brings a unique hybrid melee-caster playstyle. This Tempest Wind build leverages the Monk's powerful combo system for exceptional clear and boss damage.

**Key Strengths:**
- Engaging combo-based gameplay
- Excellent AoE clear with wind skills
- Strong defenses with Evasion + Energy Shield
- High mobility with dodge and dash

**Core Playstyle:**
Build combo points with basic attacks, spend them on powerful wind abilities, and weave between enemies using your superior mobility.

## Skill Setup

### Act 1-3 Skills

| Slot | Skill Gem | Support Gems |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Main | Wind Blast | Area Damage, Concentrated Effect |
| Combo | Fist of Wind | Faster Attacks, Added Lightning |
| Movement | Whirlwind Dash | Faster Casting, Second Wind |
| Defense | Wind Ward | Increased Duration, Arcane Surge |

### Act 4+ Upgrades

- Unlock **Tempest Strike** for maximum AoE
- Add **Cyclone** for pack clearing
- Use **Mantra of Storms** for boss bursting

## Passive Tree Priority

1. **Combo nodes** - Increase max combo and combo gain
2. **Elemental damage** - Wind and lightning nodes
3. **Evasion** - Convert to armor with Iron Reflexes
4. **Energy Shield** - Hybrid defense scaling
5. **Attack speed** - Faster combos = more damage

## Gear Recommendations

### Weapons
- **Early (1-40):** Quarterstaff with added elemental damage
- **Mid (40-60):** Slicing Quarterstaff with crit chance
- **Late (60+):** Temple Quarterstaff with high phys DPS

### Armor
- Hybrid Evasion/ES bases
- Look for "combo gain" on gloves
- Movement speed on boots is mandatory

### Jewels
- Combo generation speed
- Elemental damage with attack speed

## Gameplay Tips

### Combo Management
Always maintain 5 combo points before spending. Use basic attacks to generate between packs. The Monk's power is in its resource management.

### Positioning
Use Whirlwind Dash to position behind enemies for bonus damage. Flanking is a core mechanic for the Monk.

### Boss Strategy
- Build 5 combo points before entering boss arena
- Use Wind Blast to stack damage debuffs
- Save Mantra of Storms for boss phases
- Dodge roll through telegraphed attacks

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the best ascendancy?
Invoker provides the best balance of offense and defense for this build. Ascendant is good for hybrid playstyles.

### Is Monk good for beginners?
The Monk has a higher skill floor than Witch or Ranger but offers engaging gameplay. Recommended for players who enjoy active combat.

### What's the damage type focus?
Wind and Lightning damage. Stack "elemental damage" and "area damage" for the best returns.

### Can I use this for endgame?
Yes, the build scales well into late mapping with proper gear. Transition to crit based scaling around level 85.
