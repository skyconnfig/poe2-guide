# Introduction

Starting Path of Exile 2 can be overwhelming. With multiple classes, an enormous passive tree, and complex systems, choosing the right build for your first playthrough is critical. This guide breaks down the top 5 beginner-friendly builds that offer forgiving gameplay, smooth progression, and excellent introduction to POE2's mechanics.

---

# 1. Witch — Minion Summoner

**Difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆**

The Witch is the most recommended beginner class in POE2. Minions do the fighting while you focus on positioning and support.

**Why it's great for beginners:**
- Minions tank damage for you
- Very safe ranged gameplay
- Low gear requirements
- Forgiving progression

**Playstyle:** Summon minions, apply curses, collect loot.

---

# 2. Warrior — Slam Build

**Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆**

The Warrior's high survivability makes it extremely forgiving. You can afford to make positioning mistakes.

**Why it's great for beginners:**
- Highest base survivability
- Very forgiving playstyle
- Simple skill rotation
- Easy to understand gear

**Playstyle:** Use slam skills, warcries for buffs, and facetank dangerous enemies.

---

# 3. Ranger — Lightning Arrow

**Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆**

Fast clearing and safe distance combat make the Ranger an excellent choice for new players.

**Why it's great for beginners:**
- Kill enemies before they reach you
- Fast movement speed
- Simple skill targeting
- Excellent for learning boss mechanics

**Playstyle:** Shoot arrows, maintain distance, use mobility skills.

---

# 4. Sorceress — Elemental Caster

**Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆**

Screen-wide explosions and strong crowd control make the Sorceress satisfying for new players.

**Why it's great for beginners:**
- Massive area damage
- Freeze keeps you safe
- Low gear dependency
- Satisfying gameplay

**Playstyle:** Cast spells, freeze enemies, reposition with Flame Dash.

---

# 5. Monk — Hybrid Fighter

**Difficulty: ★★★☆☆**

The Monk requires more active play but offers the most engaging combat experience for new players.

**Why it's great for beginners:**
- Strong hybrid defenses
- Engaging combat system
- Good mobility
- Rewarding skill progression

**Playstyle:** Build combo points, spend on powerful attacks, weave between enemies.

# Beginner Tips for All Builds

1. **Cap your resistances** — The single most important defensive stat
2. **Life on every gear piece** — Prioritize life above damage early
3. **Use a movement skill** — Always have Flame Dash, Dash, or Leap Slam equipped
4. **Upgrade your weapon often** — Weapon damage is your primary DPS source
5. **Follow a build guide** — The passive tree is too complex to navigate blind

---

# FAQ

**What's the easiest class in Path of Exile 2?** The Witch with a minion build is the easiest class for complete beginners.

**Can I change my build later?** Yes, you can respec passive points using gold or orbs of regret.
