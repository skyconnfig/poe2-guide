# Introduction

Looking for the best Witch leveling build in Path of Exile 2? This guide covers a fast, beginner-friendly setup that makes campaign progression smooth while keeping your damage high and survivability consistent.

The Witch is one of the strongest early-game classes in POE2 thanks to powerful minions, reliable spell damage, and safe ranged gameplay. Whether you're completely new to Path of Exile 2 or simply want a fast leveling experience for league start, this build offers excellent clear speed without requiring expensive gear.

This Path of Exile 2 Witch leveling build focuses on Summon Raging Spirits and Chaos-based spell support, allowing you to scale damage naturally while staying mobile throughout the campaign.

---

# Why This Build Is Strong

The Witch leveling build excels because it combines high damage output with low mechanical complexity. Most enemies die before reaching you, while your minions absorb pressure during difficult encounters.

Here's why many players consider this one of the best Witch leveling builds in POE2:

- Extremely smooth campaign progression
- Strong early-game damage scaling
- Safe ranged combat style
- Minimal gear dependency
- Excellent boss uptime
- Beginner-friendly mechanics
- Easy transition into endgame builds

Unlike many melee-focused leveling builds, the Witch can comfortably clear large packs while avoiding dangerous mechanics. This makes it especially effective for hardcore players and first-time Path of Exile 2 players.

Another major advantage is flexibility. You can easily transition this setup into multiple endgame archetypes, including Chaos DoT, Minion Army, or Hybrid Necromancer builds.

# Skill Setup

## Core Skills

### Summon Raging Spirits

This is your primary leveling skill during the campaign. The spirits aggressively seek enemies and deal strong fire damage while keeping combat safe from a distance.

Recommended support gems:
- Minion Damage
- Feeding Frenzy
- Infernal Legion
- Unleash

Summon Raging Spirits provides excellent screen clear and scales naturally through gem levels alone.

### Chaos Bolt

Chaos Bolt acts as your supplemental ranged spell for tougher rares and bosses.

Recommended supports:
- Controlled Destruction
- Void Manipulation
- Added Chaos Damage

Chaos damage performs very well during early progression because many campaign enemies have limited resistance against it.

### Bone Cage

Bone Cage is your primary defensive utility skill. Use it to trap aggressive enemies, create spacing during boss fights, and improve survivability in crowded encounters.

### Flame Dash

Mobility is essential in Path of Exile 2. Flame Dash allows you to dodge boss mechanics, cross terrain quickly, and maintain fast leveling speed.

# Gameplay Tips

- Maintain Momentum: avoid full-clearing every zone or over-farming early acts
- Keep Minions Active: resummon spirits before encounters
- Upgrade Flasks Regularly: life, mana, and utility flasks
- Prioritize Resistances Before Bosses: check fire, cold, lightning resistance before major encounters
