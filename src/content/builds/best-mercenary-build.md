# Introduction

The Mercenary in Path of Exile 2 combines crossbow ranged attacks with tactical traps and support skills. This hybrid ranged build offers strong area control, consistent damage output, and excellent utility that shines in both solo and group play.

With high versatility and strong defensive options, the Mercenary is an excellent choice for players who want options in every combat situation.

# Gameplay Tips

- Pre-place Explosive Traps before engaging packs
- Use Battle Standard to boost damage in tough fights
- Maintain distance with Roll cooldown management
- Combine Penetrating Shot with traps for burst damage on rares
